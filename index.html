Our Story

Your Look, Perfected

The Makeoverhouse is Etobicoke's destination for premium hair and grooming. We're a full-service salon and barber shop under one roof, serving clients who expect nothing less than excellence.

Whether you're booking a colour transformation, a precision cut, or a fresh fade — our team of skilled stylists and barbers brings artistry and attention to every appointment. With over 315 five-star Google reviews, our reputation speaks for itself.

Conveniently located inside Martin Grove Mall at 2200 Martin Grove Rd, with free parking and walk-ins always welcome.
